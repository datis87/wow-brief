# Digest 2026-11-26-1501 UTC

## Class changes

[Druid] Balance Druid Talent and Ability Revisions
Hurricane now hits correctly, Starlight Wrath reduces Starfire cast time, Moon and Stars grants Starfire pushback resistance, and Dreamstate replaces Moonfury. Moonkin Form now provides a 10% Arcane and Nature damage buff, and Eclipse increases Wrath critical strike chance.
So what: Review your talent tree as points have been reset and damage output is now tied more heavily to Moonkin Form.
https://www.wowhead.com/news=384000/fixes-for-legacy-points-classes-and-quests-wow-forever-hotfixes-for-november

[Hunter] Beast Mastery and Pet Critical Strike Fixes
Fixed a bug where pets had 0% critical strike chance on melee attacks and Summoned Hawks had 5% less critical strike chance than intended. Summoned Hawks now follow attack logic regardless of pet passivity and can be dismissed via pet bar.
So what: Expect your pet and Summoned Hawk damage to increase due to the restored critical strike chance.
https://www.wowhead.com/news=384000/fixes-for-legacy-points-classes-and-quests-wow-forever-hotfixes-for-november

[Paladin] Exorcism and Judgement Hotfixes
Exorcism can now critically strike, and Judgement debuffs will no longer fail to refresh from attacks if the remaining duration drops below 20 seconds. Additionally, the Oath of Light duration is increased to 60 minutes and riding skill bugs were addressed.
So what: Paladins gain increased damage potential and improved quality of life for debuff maintenance and mounting.
https://www.wowhead.com/news=384000/fixes-for-legacy-points-classes-and-quests-wow-forever-hotfixes-for-november

[Rogue] Poisons and Stealth Ability Fixes
Rogue Poisons and Blinding Powder are temporarily set to bind-on-pickup while a bug is addressed. A fix was also applied to ensure the Poised talent is correctly removed when casting Sap or Distract from Stealth.
So what: Be aware that you cannot currently trade or sell poisons until the hotfix is removed.
https://www.wowhead.com/news=384000/fixes-for-legacy-points-classes-and-quests-wow-forever-hotfixes-for-november

[Shaman] Chain Lightning and Maelstrom Weapon Fix
A fix was applied to prevent Chain Lightning from consuming Clearcasting if the Shaman simultaneously has 5 stacks of Maelstrom Weapon.
So what: Expect more consistent resource management when using Maelstrom Weapon procs.
https://www.wowhead.com/news=384000/fixes-for-legacy-points-classes-and-quests-wow-forever-hotfixes-for-november

[Warlock] Pet Critical Strike and Riding Skill Fixes
Warlock pets now have the intended critical strike chance for melee attacks, and a bug causing Succubus and Incubus to have 5% higher critical strike chance than intended was corrected. Riding skill issues were also resolved.
So what: Warlock pet damage output has been normalized.
https://www.wowhead.com/news=384000/fixes-for-legacy-points-classes-and-quests-wow-forever-hotfixes-for-november

[Warrior] Arms Warrior Talent Fix
The Weaponmaster talent will no longer fail to reapply correctly after a Warrior dies in Battlegrounds.
So what: Arms Warriors no longer need to worry about losing their talent bonus after a respawn in PvP.
https://www.wowhead.com/news=384000/fixes-for-legacy-points-classes-and-quests-wow-forever-hotfixes-for-november

[General] Miscellaneous System and Dungeon Hotfixes
General fixes include Alterac Valley adjustments, nerfs to the Relevation Enchant, and itemization fixes for Unerring Purpose, Blackblade of Shahram, and Blackhand's Breadth. Dungeon interaction fixes were applied to Blackfathom Deeps and the Temple of Atal'Hakkar.
So what: Check your gear and quest logs to see if items or dungeon encounters you were struggling with have been adjusted.
